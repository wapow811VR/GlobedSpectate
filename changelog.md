# Changelog

Versions are vX.Y.Z: X for major updates, Y for minor updates, Z for bug fixes.

## v1.1.2
- Added the GitHub and Ko-fi buttons to the mod page
- Added credit for Globed and its creators to the description

## v1.1.1
- Went back to the game's own camera while spectating; the camera changes from v1.1.0 are removed
- Added a mod logo

## v1.1.0
- The song now follows the player you are watching: it starts from where they are when you begin spectating mid level or switch player, goes quiet while they are dead, and picks up from where they respawn (including practice mode checkpoints)
- Switching to another player without stopping now does a full stop first, then starts on the new player
- Fixed the camera losing the player after teleport portals and on levels with random triggers
- Fixed the camera not following in platformer levels

## v1.0.1
- Fixed the spectate button not showing in the pause menu
- Fixed the keybinds not working, and gave them defaults: `]` next player, `[` previous player, `Backspace` stop

## v1.0.0
- Initial release
